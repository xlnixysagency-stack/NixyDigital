Cookie Policy

Last Updated: February 15, 2026

Quick Summary: We use cookies to keep you logged in, remember your preferences, and understand how you use our site. You can control cookies in your browser settings, but some features won't work without them.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and understand how you use the site.

Cookies can be:

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Cookies that remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them
  • First-Party Cookies: Set by Study Buddy AI directly
  • Third-Party Cookies: Set by services we use (like Firebase or analytics tools)

2. How We Use Cookies

Study Buddy AI uses cookies for the following purposes:

2.1 Essential Cookies (Required)

These cookies are necessary for the website to function. Without them, you cannot use core features like logging in or saving your work.

2.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences and settings to provide a personalized experience.

2.3 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Authentication Keeps you logged into your Study Buddy AI account Session or 30 days
Preferences Remembers your settings (theme, language, etc.) 1 year
Firebase Manages user authentication and data storage Varies (set by Firebase)
Usage Analytics Tracks how you use features to improve the platform 1 year
Performance Monitors site performance and load times Session

4. Third-Party Cookies

We use third-party services that may set their own cookies:

4.1 Firebase (Google)

  • Purpose: User authentication, database, and hosting
  • Cookies Set: Authentication tokens, session management
  • Privacy Policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4.2 Groq AI

  • Purpose: AI processing for study assistance
  • Cookies Set: API session tokens (if any)
  • Privacy Policy: groq.com/privacy-policy/

5. How to Control Cookies

5.1 Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • Block all cookies
  • Block third-party cookies only
  • Delete cookies after each session
  • Set exceptions for specific websites

How to manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and Site Permissions

5.2 Impact of Disabling Cookies

Warning: If you disable essential cookies, some features of Study Buddy AI will not work properly:

  • You won't be able to stay logged in
  • Your preferences won't be saved
  • Some features may not load correctly
  • Your study progress may not be tracked

6. Local Storage and Similar Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use other browser storage technologies:

6.1 Local Storage

Used to store larger amounts of data locally (like draft essays or study notes) so you don't lose your work if you close the browser.

6.2 Session Storage

Temporary storage that clears when you close the tab, used for short-term data like form inputs.

6.3 IndexedDB

Used by Firebase and other services to store structured data locally for better performance.

7. Do Not Track (DNT)

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. Currently, there is no industry standard for how to respond to DNT signals. We do not track users across third-party websites, but we do use cookies on our own site as described in this policy.

8. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology or regulations. When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • Notify you via email if you have an account
  • Display a notice on our website

9. Your Consent

By using Study Buddy AI, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy. If you do not agree, you can disable cookies in your browser, but this may limit your ability to use certain features.
